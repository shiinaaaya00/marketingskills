# Product PRD Generation

The PRD is downstream of verified product truth.

## Purpose

Define how the product should be positioned, presented, launched, measured, and accepted by downstream teams.

It is not the primary product fact store.

## Decision statuses

- `DECIDED`
- `PROPOSED`
- `OPEN`

## Recommended sections

1. Product overview
2. Product objective
3. Target customer
4. Customer problem / JTBD
5. Positioning
6. Core value proposition
7. Verified selling points
8. Secondary selling points
9. Use cases
10. Variant / SKU strategy
11. Bundle / included-item strategy
12. Pricing and commercial rules
13. Packaging
14. PDP requirements
15. Product media requirements
16. Product video requirements
17. SEO/GEO requirements
18. EDM/launch messaging
19. Marketplace/affiliate/influencer
20. Logistics/inventory constraints
21. Compliance/prohibited claims
22. Analytics/measurement
23. Launch readiness gates
24. Acceptance criteria
25. Open decisions

If the PRD proposes a new price, bundle, feature, accessory, or claim, keep it `PROPOSED`. Do not insert it into PRODUCT_MASTER as `LOCKED` until verified.
