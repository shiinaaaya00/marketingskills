# Extraction Rules

Extract; do not editorialize.

Preserve:
- exact product names;
- model/SKU;
- units;
- qualifier wording;
- market/variant scope;
- source revision/date.

Do not infer missing units or performance.

## Images can support

Visible shape, finish, controls, ports/openings, labels/logo location, clearly visible packaging/items.

## Images do not by themselves verify

Exact dimensions, noise, runtime, power, coverage, safety, certifications, material composition, therapeutic/health benefit.

## Notes/chats

Treat as working evidence unless explicitly confirmed as final.
