# Product Master QA

## Identity
- Official product name known.
- Internal model/SKU separated from marketing name.
- Variant and market scope explicit.

## Facts
- Locked facts have sources.
- Units present where needed.
- Derived values include formula/inputs.
- Unverified values are not presented as locked.
- Conflicts remain visible.

## Claims
- Approved claims reference supporting facts.
- Channel permissions explicit.
- Prohibited claims flow into downstream handoff.
- Qualifiers preserved exactly.

## Assets
- Approved assets identifiable.
- Variant/view recorded.
- Reference-only assets not treated as publishable.
- AI concepts not mistaken for product truth.

## PRD
- Strategy derives from current facts.
- Proposed decisions do not become facts silently.
- Open decisions visible.
- Acceptance criteria testable.

## Readiness
- Blockers identify affected downstream tasks.
- Current version/status explicit.
- Handoff reflects current master version.
