# Claims Rules

A fact is not automatically a marketing claim.

## Statuses

`APPROVED`, `APPROVED_WITH_QUALIFIER`, `INTERNAL_ONLY`, `UNVERIFIED`, `PROHIBITED`, `SUPERSEDED`.

## Track

Claim ID, exact wording, supporting fact IDs, evidence, status, allowed/prohibited channels, qualifier, market restrictions, notes.

## Channels

PDP, Homepage, Video, SEO, EDM, Paid Ads, Marketplace, Affiliate, Influencer, Packaging, Customer Support.

## High-risk areas

Health/wellness, environmental, safety, child/pet safety, medical, allergy, therapeutic, energy efficiency, durability/lifetime, "best"/"#1", "clinically proven", "non-toxic", coverage/range, noise, performance comparison, sustainability.

Without substantiation, use `UNVERIFIED` or `PROHIBITED`.
