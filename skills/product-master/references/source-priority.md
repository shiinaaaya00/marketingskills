# Source Priority

## Default authority tiers

### Tier 1 — approved primary truth
Final engineering/spec document, approved CAD/dimensional drawing, approved packaging artwork, approved compliance document, approved ERP/PIM product master, signed-off BOM.

### Tier 2 — current operational systems
Current SKU/inventory export, current Shopify/admin listing, approved cost sheet, current warehouse/3PL master, current official manual.

### Tier 3 — approved commercial material
Approved PDP copy, launch brief, product deck, sales sheet.

### Tier 4 — approved visual evidence
Approved product photography, approved packaging photography, explicitly production-equivalent prototype photography.

### Tier 5 — working material
Draft spreadsheet, chat notes, meeting notes, supplier email, draft copy, unapproved mockup.

### Tier 6 — external reference only
Competitor pages, reviews, inspiration images, reference videos, market research.

Tier 6 may guide strategy but never verifies this product's facts.

## Rules

- A newer approved revision may supersede an older source.
- Market-specific facts may coexist when scoped correctly.
- Variant-specific facts must not be generalized.
- A photo may verify visible geometry, not technical performance.
