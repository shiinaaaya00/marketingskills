# Versioning

## Master version

Suggested:
- `1.0.0`: first approved master.
- patch `1.0.1`: metadata/typo fix with no downstream meaning change.
- minor `1.1.0`: new verified facts/assets/claims.
- major `2.0.0`: material product revision or substantial spec/package change.

## Change log

Record date, version, type, changed ID/field, old value, new value, source, reason, downstream impact.

## Downstream impact examples

- dimension change -> PDP, image, video review;
- price change -> PDP, EDM, ads review;
- included-item change -> PDP, video, image, support review;
- SKU change -> Shopify, inventory, logistics, marketplace review;
- claim status change -> all public marketing channels review.
