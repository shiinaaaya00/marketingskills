# Asset Rules

## Asset classes
Product packshot, lifestyle image, detail image, packaging image, dimension diagram, instruction image, accessory image, label artwork, logo, 3D/CAD, video, generated concept, reference/inspiration.

## Statuses

- `APPROVED`
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`
- `NEEDS_RETOUCH`
- `NEEDS_REVIEW`
- `OBSOLETE`
- `PROHIBITED`

## AI-generated assets

Generated product images/videos are not automatically approved.

Record:
- source references used;
- whether geometry is faithful;
- label/logo accuracy;
- included-item accuracy;
- approval status.

## Asset IDs

Use `AST-001`, `AST-002`, ...

## Metadata

Asset ID, filename/path, type, variant, view/purpose, dimensions, status, source, approved channels, restrictions, notes.
