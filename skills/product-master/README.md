# product-master

Reusable Codex Skill that turns raw product material into a controlled Single Source of Truth plus an execution PRD.

**PRODUCT_MASTER = what the product is.**

**PRODUCT_PRD = how the product should be positioned, presented, launched, and accepted.**

## Inputs

Specs, engineering files, spreadsheets, product images, packaging, BOM/cost, SKU/inventory, listings, manuals, approved copy, compliance files, research, and notes.

## Outputs

- PRODUCT_MASTER.md
- product_master.json
- PRODUCT_PRD.md
- CLAIMS_MATRIX.md
- ASSET_INDEX.md
- SOURCE_INVENTORY.md
- SOURCE_AUDIT.md
- LAUNCH_READINESS.md
- CHANGELOG.md
- downstream_handoff.json
