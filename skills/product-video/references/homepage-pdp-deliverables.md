# Homepage and PDP Deliverables

These are defaults, not hard requirements. Adapt to the site's real layout and platform.

## Shared master

Purpose: source edit containing the best approved shots.

Default:
- 30–60 s
- 16:9
- high-quality master
- optional music/sound
- minimal baked-in text

## Homepage desktop hero

Purpose: immediate mood + product recognition.

Default:
- 6–15 s
- 16:9 or the site's actual hero ratio
- muted autoplay
- loopable
- minimal cuts
- no essential audio dependency
- preserve negative space for HTML headline/CTA
- avoid tiny baked-in text

Preferred content:
- reveal;
- hero form;
- one signature function or sensory moment;
- lifestyle/brand close.

## Homepage mobile hero

Purpose: same campaign idea, recomposed for narrow screens.

Default:
- 6–15 s
- 9:16 or actual mobile hero ratio
- independent framing, not blind center-crop
- product and key action remain inside mobile safe zone

## Homepage product module

Default:
- 10–25 s
- 16:9, 4:5, or module-specific ratio
- may explain one or two benefits
- compatible with muted playback

## PDP main/gallery video

Purpose: help a shopper understand what the product is and why it matters.

Default:
- 15–45 s
- 16:9 or 4:5 depending gallery
- clearer than Homepage hero
- may include concise on-screen benefit copy
- show product, function, operation, and included items when relevant

## PDP feature loops

Purpose: one function per loop.

Default:
- 4–10 s
- 1:1 or 4:5, depending section
- one benefit only
- easy to understand without audio
- loop where practical

Examples:
- light behavior;
- mist/output behavior;
- button/remote interaction;
- refill/installation;
- texture/material;
- included-set reveal.

## Poster frames

Create a poster frame for each web video:
- same visual identity as the first frame;
- strong product legibility;
- no mid-transition frame;
- text optional;
- crop-safe.

## Web performance

Set a target budget per placement before export.
For large autoplay heroes, aggressively optimize duration and bitrate while preserving perceived quality.

Do not promise a fixed file size until actual encoding is measured.

For Shopify or similar storefronts:
- muted autoplay;
- `playsinline`;
- loop only when the sequence is designed for it;
- lazy-load non-hero videos when the theme implementation allows;
- avoid making video the only way to understand a critical product fact.
