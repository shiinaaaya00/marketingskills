# Shot Design Rules

## One shot, one job

Every AI-generated shot should have one primary purpose.

Bad:
- reveal product, rotate 360°, open the lid, insert oil, show mist, turn on light, then transition to a living room in 6 seconds.

Better:
- Shot A: product reveal.
- Shot B: material macro.
- Shot C: installation.
- Shot D: output/mist.
- Shot E: lifestyle.

## Shot families

### 1. Reveal
Establish silhouette and premium feel.

### 2. Hero form
Show full product cleanly and accurately.

### 3. Material/detail
Use macro framing for texture, metal, fabric, glass, controls, seams.

### 4. Function
Show a verified function at the real physical location.

### 5. Operation
Human hand, remote, button, refill, attachment, setup.

### 6. Set/variant
Show included accessories, colorways, or bundle components accurately.

### 7. Lifestyle
Place the product in a context that matches target customer and brand positioning.

### 8. Brand close
Product name, logo, quiet final pose, or loop bridge.

## Composition

Specify:
- product center/left/right;
- approximate frame occupancy;
- eye-level/top-down/low-angle;
- negative space;
- crop-safe region.

## Camera language

Use restrained, commercial moves:
- slow dolly in/out;
- gentle orbit;
- slider;
- macro rack focus;
- tilt/pan;
- controlled handheld only when brand-appropriate.

Avoid:
- arbitrary spins;
- extreme parallax;
- impossible crane moves in tiny interiors;
- aggressive speed ramps unless justified.

## Functional visualization

Prefer literal physical demonstration.

When a technical mechanism is not visible and no CAD is provided:
- use tasteful external graphic overlays;
- use abstract particles/paths clearly presented as visualization;
- do not fabricate internal engineering.

## Human interaction

When hands/people appear:
- define the exact action;
- keep product geometry locked;
- keep fingers away from areas that would be physically impossible;
- do not add unverified ergonomic claims.

## Continuity

Create a continuity note for each shot:
- variant;
- orientation;
- lid/button state;
- light state;
- accessory state;
- fluid/refill state;
- environment;
- time of day.
