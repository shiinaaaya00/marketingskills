# Source of Truth Rules

## Priority

Use the most authoritative supplied source for each fact.

Typical priority:
1. official product engineering/specification document;
2. approved product master / PIM / ERP export;
3. CAD / dimensional drawings;
4. approved packaging artwork;
5. approved PDP copy;
6. approved product photography;
7. working notes;
8. reference videos from other brands.

A reference video is NEVER evidence for this product's feature set.

## Fact classes

### LOCKED
Verified and safe to depict or state.

### VISUAL_ONLY
Visible in approved images but not confirmed as a technical claim. May be depicted visually but not converted into a written claim.

### UNVERIFIED
Mentioned in incomplete notes or conflicting sources. Do not state as fact.

### PROHIBITED
Explicitly disallowed, inaccurate, obsolete, or legally risky.

## Conflict handling

Create a conflict table:
- field;
- source A;
- source B;
- conflict;
- safe action.

Do not silently reconcile conflicting dimensions, colors, included items, performance numbers, safety claims, or certifications.

## Product identity lock

Record:
- product name;
- SKU/model;
- variant;
- dimensions;
- silhouette;
- top/bottom shape;
- button count and placement;
- port positions;
- light-emitting areas;
- mist/output location;
- seams/joints;
- materials;
- logo location;
- included accessories.

These are continuity constraints for every keyframe and video shot.
